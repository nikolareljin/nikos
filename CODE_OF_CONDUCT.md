# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part in NikOS a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with people new to Linux, Ansible or AI tools
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- giving and accepting constructive feedback on the work, not the person
- owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The maintainer may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
Discussions, reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct to the maintainer,
[@nikolareljin](https://github.com/nikolareljin), using the contact options on
that GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature on any comment, issue or pull request. Reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For unprofessional or unwelcome language or behaviour: a
   private, written warning explaining the problem; a public apology may be
   asked for.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or repeated behaviour: a warning
   with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the
   people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation: a temporary ban from any
   interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression
   towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban.
